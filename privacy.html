Privacy Policy for AmpPlan Last updated: August 13, 2026 AmpPlan: Inverter & Battery ("AmpPlan") is provided by SideQuest Works. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the AmpPlan Android application. 1. Information AmpPlan Stores Locally AmpPlan is designed primarily as an offline utility application. Information such as battery and inverter calculation inputs, appliance selections, custom appliances, preferences, and saved plans may be stored locally on your device so that the app can function and retain your settings. AmpPlan does not require you to create an account or sign in to use its core functionality. The developer does not operate a cloud database for storing your AmpPlan plans or calculations. 2. Advertising AmpPlan uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements. Google Mobile Ads may automatically process information including: IP address app and advertisement interactions diagnostic information device identifiers advertising identifiers where available and permitted This information may be used by Google and its advertising partners for purposes such as advertising, ad measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention. Advertising behavior may vary depending on your region, device settings, applicable laws, and privacy choices. For more information about Google's privacy practices, please review Google's Privacy Policy. 3. Consent and Privacy Choices AmpPlan uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) where required to present privacy and consent choices. Users in regions where consent or privacy choices are required may be shown a Google-managed privacy message. Where applicable, AmpPlan also provides a Privacy choices option in the app's Settings screen so users can review or change available advertising privacy choices. 4. Location Information AmpPlan does not request access to your device's precise GPS location for its inverter or battery calculations. Advertising services may use information such as your IP address to estimate a general geographic area as part of advertising and related services. 5. Sharing AmpPlan allows you to share calculation results or saved-plan information using Android's standard sharing functionality. Sharing occurs only when you choose to initiate it. When you share information with another application or service, the privacy practices of that third-party application or service apply to the information you choose to share. 6. Internet and Offline Use AmpPlan's core inverter, battery, appliance, charging-time, converter, and saved-plan functions are designed to work offline. An internet connection may be used for services such as advertising and privacy/consent messaging. If advertising is unavailable, AmpPlan's core calculation functionality remains available. 7. Third-Party Services AmpPlan uses third-party services including: Google AdMob Google Mobile Ads SDK Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) These services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. 8. Data Security AmpPlan is designed to minimize the amount of information handled by the developer by keeping core plans and calculations on the user's device. Third-party services used by the app may transmit information securely according to their own security and privacy practices. 9. Children's Privacy AmpPlan is a general-purpose inverter, battery, and electrical planning utility and is not designed specifically for children. 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when AmpPlan's features, advertising implementation, third-party services, or applicable requirements change. Any updated policy will be published at this same privacy-policy URL with an updated revision date. 11.
